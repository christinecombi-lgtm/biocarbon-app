🔬 Factory QC Testing Protocols & Grading Criteria Reference

Use these strict physical and laboratory standards to classify your finished 400kg/hr briquette runs into Grade A (Export), Grade B (Local BBQ), or Rejected.

⭐ Export Grade A
  • Drop Test Retain: > 95% (1.8m drop)
  • Moisture Content: < 5.0%
  • Ignition Time: < 3.0 mins
  • Burn Time: > 75–85 mins
  • Fixed Carbon: ≥ 75%
  • Smoke / Odor: Smokeless, odorless white ash
⚡ Standard Grade B
  • Drop Test Retain: 90% – 95%
  • Moisture Content: 5.0% – 8.0%
  • Ignition Time: 3.0 – 4.0 mins
  • Burn Time: 50 – 70 mins
  • Fixed Carbon: 65% – 75%
  • Application: Local commercial grill / BBQ use
❌ Rejected / Re-run
  • Drop Retain: < 90% (Crumbles easily)
  • Moisture Content: > 8.0% (Sizzles on ignition)
  • Burn Time: < 50 mins
  • Defect: Excessive smoke, tar odor, or uncarbonized core pockets
  • Action: Crush back to raw char stock

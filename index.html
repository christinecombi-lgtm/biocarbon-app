🔬 Factory QC Testing Protocols & Biochar Evaluation Standards

Scientific evaluation standards for charcoal briquettes and agricultural biochar (raw vs. nutrient-charged) derived from agricultural research (Lehmann & Joseph, 2009; IBI Standards).

⭐ Charcoal Briquette Standards (Export Grade A / Local B)
  • Drop Test Retain: > 95% (Export) / 90% (Local)
  • Moisture Content: < 5.0% (Export) / < 8.0% (Local)
  • Ignition & Burn Time: < 3 mins ignition, > 75 mins burn time
  • Fixed Carbon: ≥ 75% (Export) / 65-75% (Local BBQ)
🌱 Rice Husk Biochar Standards (Raw vs Charged)
  • Surface Area (BET): > 250 m²/g (High porosity for microbial habitat)
  • pH Range: 7.0 – 9.0 (Effective acid soil neutralizer)
  • Raw Biochar: High carbon stability (>80%), ready for composting/charging
  • Charged Biochar: Pre-inoculated with amino acids, organic fertilizer, or compost tea (Cation Exchange Capacity enriched)

💡 Smart Business Owner's Guide: Accounting for Farm Usage & Sales

To maintain accurate financial statements and profitability metrics for Combinido Biocarbon Charcoal Manufacturing while utilizing biochar and wood vinegar on your farm experiments:

  • Internal Transfer Pricing: Record farm-applied biochar and wood vinegar as internal transfers from inventory to an "Agricultural R&D / Farm Experiment Expense" account at cost of production.
  • Revenue Recognition: When wood vinegar or biochar is sold commercially to external farmers or online, record the transaction in Sales Dispatches at prevailing market rates.
  • Cost Roll-up: The unit cost per kilo below automatically incorporates direct raw materials, labor wages, operational overhead, and government statutory contributions.
